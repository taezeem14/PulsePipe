GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sine really 100% free with no ads? ▾

Yes, absolutely. Sine is an open-source project released under the GPL-3.0 license. There are no subscriptions, no locked tiers, no pop-ups, and no banner ads. SponsorBlock is built-in to skip sponsored intros and podcast promos.

How does Sine achieve 1.2s cold start without buffering? ▾

Standard scrapers wait for YouTube's full DASH video/audio manifest and decipher encrypted cipher signatures. Sine prioritizes standalone AAC audio (itag 140) and mobile client manifests, connecting via persistent HTTP/2 pools straight to Android's hardware audio decoder.

Where are downloaded songs stored on my phone? ▾

In v1.0.7, downloaded tracks and videos are saved directly to /storage/emulated/0/Sine-Music in your device's root internal storage, right next to your DCIM and Download folders. You can access and copy your music freely anytime.

How do the Vocals Only and Instruments Only filters work? ▾

The Sound Shaper applies hardware DSP notch and shelf equalization to the audio output in real time. Vocals Only boosts the 1 kHz – 4 kHz vocal frequency bands while attenuating low bass and high cymbals. Instruments Only suppresses mid-band voice harmonics to create a karaoke backing track.
